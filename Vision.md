# Core Vision

A living space simulation where the player exists inside a large
universe that continues changing independently of them.

The player is not the center of the galaxy.

The universe feels alive because things are actually happening whether
the player participates or not.

The player begins small and slowly grows into a larger role without ever
fully controlling the galaxy.

The intended fantasy is long-term independence, operational growth,
meaningful choices, and living inside a universe that does not revolve
around the player.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# What The Player Experiences

The player experiences:

-   politics
-   trade
-   shortages
-   conflict
-   changing news
-   rumors
-   faction tension
-   opportunity
-   danger
-   long-term consequences

The universe should regularly create situations that make the player
ask:

*"What is happening here?"*

*"Can I profit from this?"*

*"Should I get involved?"*

*"Who benefits from this?"*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Universe Feel

The universe should feel:

-   alive
-   imperfectly known
-   politically active
-   economically pressured
-   sometimes dangerous
-   occasionally surprising

The player should often feel:

*"Things happened while I was away."*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Factions and Conflict

The galaxy contains multiple major powers, competing interests, and
unstable relationships.

No faction is intended to be perfectly heroic or purely villainous.

Earth exists as a dominant human authority balancing security, politics,
economics, diplomacy, and internal pressure.

Mars exists as a weaker underground movement seeking support,
legitimacy, survival, and leverage against a much stronger power.

Alien civilizations are significant political actors rather than
simplistic one-species stereotypes.

One hidden force, currently conceptualized as **The Dissonance**,
influences events indirectly through destabilization, hidden agendas,
ideology, pressure, and long-term objectives.

Distance, separation, and dangerous transit matter.

Travel between powers often involves risk, uncertain regions, competing
influence, piracy, and difficult choices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Player Fantasy

The player is not a chosen hero.

The player is an ordinary person entering space life with limited
resources.

Over long periods of real play the player may grow toward:

-   trusted crews
-   larger ships
-   multiple ships
-   regional influence
-   logistics operations
-   business growth
-   fleet management
-   meaningful reputation

The fantasy is:

*"Penthouse with a view, not owner of the entire building."*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Gameplay Style

The project leans toward:

-   strategic thinking
-   operational leadership
-   hybrid control
-   crew management
-   logistics
-   economic opportunity
-   exploration
-   risk management

The game is not intended to be a traditional first-person shooter.

Ground operations, when present, lean toward management, tactical
decisions, operational preparation, crew quality, and consequences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Example: Interconnected Systems Philosophy

The project's systems are intended to influence one another rather than
operate in isolation.

Example scenario:

The player is operating in a distant system and lacks direct visibility
into Sol.

Through news reports, rumors, intelligence, or delayed information, the
player learns of rising Earth--Mars tensions and worsening food
shortages.

Curious or opportunistic, the player travels to investigate.

On arrival, the player discovers a larger crisis.

Earth is pressuring Mars to surrender underground rebel elements.

Food shipments have been reduced.

Markets are strained.

Black markets are active.

Public hardship is rising.

The player discovers multiple possible responses.

They might:

-   profit from shortages
-   smuggle food
-   support Mars
-   cooperate with Earth
-   escort cargo
-   work through black markets
-   remain neutral
-   exploit the situation for personal gain

The same situation naturally touches politics, economics, missions,
travel, skills, logistics, information, reputation, and player choice.

The intended experience is a universe where systems interact naturally
rather than feeling disconnected.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Sandbox Direction

The game is primarily a sandbox.

The universe generates situations, pressure, opportunity, shortages,
rumors, conflict, and changing conditions.

The player chooses how to respond.

Limited authored content, onboarding, or narrative arcs may exist.

However, the larger goal is a world capable of producing memorable
player stories through interacting systems.
